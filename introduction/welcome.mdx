## Introduction

Welcome! The Outlight.fun API gives you programmatic access to a powerful engine for tracking and analyzing cryptocurrency token calls across Telegram and Twitter. Use our data to build sophisticated trading tools, monitoring bots, or analytics dashboards and gain a competitive edge in the fast-paced world of crypto.
